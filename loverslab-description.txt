Lacrima - Procedural Tears
Real tears for Skyrim SE / AE: they well up under the eyes, run down the face and drip off the chin.

Every tear is simulated as fluid on the actor's own face, expression included. No tear textures, no face overlays. Lacrima is a framework: actors cry when another mod, a script, the console or the settings menu asks them to.


WHAT IT DOES

- Tears on the real face. Each eye runs a small fluid simulation on the face mesh as the game has posed it this frame, so tears follow the face's shape as the head turns and the expression changes.
- How a tear behaves. It wells up on the lower lid and clings there for a moment, then runs down as a small bead and leaves a thin wet streak that dries after a while. A drop that reaches the underside of the chin hangs there briefly before it falls.
- Several streams per eye. Tears start from up to four points along the lower lid, from the inner corner beside the nose to the outer corner, each a little to one side of the last, so trails build up over time instead of retracing one line.
- Running mascara (optional). Each tear leaves a dark streak down the exact path it ran, which builds up as the actor keeps crying, outlives the water and fades after a few minutes. A hand wiping the face smears it. Off by default; other mods can turn it on per actor.
- Wet eyes. While an actor cries, their eyes glisten.
- Hands wipe tears. A crying actor's own fingers and palms take away the tears they touch, in any animation that brings a hand to the face. There is also a wipe gesture you can trigger (the vanilla brow wipe, or any idle you pass in).
- Player and NPCs alike, on vanilla and high-poly heads.
- Light on performance. A tear at rest costs nothing until an expression, a tilt of the head or a hand stirs it, only the skin around the tears is read each frame, actors far from the camera keep their tears paused, and the number of actors crying at once is capped.

INSTALLATION

Install with a mod manager (MO2 recommended). Lacrima is one SKSE plugin plus meshes, textures, two small scripts and a config file. There is no ESP, nothing to build and nothing to configure first.

To see it working: open the console, click an NPC (or click nothing for the player) and type
    tears cry 0.8 30

Updating: install the new version over the old one. Your saved settings are kept. No clean save needed.


OPTIONAL ADD-ON: VICTIMS CRY IN SEXLAB SCENES

Two optional files make Lacrima react to SexLab on its own; install the one for your SexLab alongside Lacrima. During a scene, an actor SexLab marks as a victim cries while being penetrated (vaginal, anal or oral) and keeps crying, more quietly, for a while after it stops or the scene ends. Partners who are not victims never cry from it.

- "Lacrima add-on - SexLab P+ victims cry while penetrated", for SexLab P+. On P+ 2.19 or newer it follows P+'s live penetration detection, so the tears start and stop with what actually happens in the scene. On older P+ it goes by the stage tags.
- "Lacrima add-on - classic SexLab victims cry in penetration scenes", for classic SexLab. Classic SexLab cannot tell when a penetration happens, so victims cry through a whole vaginal, anal or oral animation. A setting lets them start only from a later stage, to skip an animation's opening stages.
- Both need Lacrima 0.1.1 or newer and PapyrusUtil, and add one small ESL-flagged plugin and two scripts.
- Settings are in Data\SKSE\Plugins\StorageUtilData\ (LacrimaSexLab.json for P+, LacrimaSexLabClassic.json for classic): on/off, how hard victims cry, how long they keep crying afterwards, whether oral counts, and whether the player and NPCs take part. Edits apply from the next scene.


SETTINGS

With SKSE Menu Framework, the Lacrima pages change everything live and save your changes when the menu closes:

- Tears - how often and how heavily the eyes shed, where tears start, how many streams per eye, how much one tear differs from the next, how many actors may cry at once.
- Look - the look style, tear size, trail width, opacity, highlight, reflection, glow, wet eyes, how long a trail stays wet, running mascara.
- Physics - how firmly tears stick, how fast they run, how far a tear runs before it stops, how long a drop clings under the chin.
- Wipe - hands taking tears away, the wipe gesture.
- Test - make the player cry, shed one tear, stop or wipe; make nearby actors cry; show where tears start; reset everything to the shipped defaults.

Without the menu, put the values you want to change into Data\SKSE\Plugins\Lacrima_User.toml, using the sections and keys of Lacrima.toml, which lists every setting with its default.

Tears look white and solid? Some ENB presets amplify materials that reflect or give off a little light of their own, which the default water look does. Set Look style to 1 (plain), and lower Opacity if they still look milky.


CONSOLE (ConsoleUtil Extended)

Commands act on the actor selected in the console, or on the player when none is selected.

- tears cry 0.7 30 - cry at intensity 0.7 for 30 seconds (0 seconds = until stopped)
- tears stop - stop crying; "tears stop 1" also wipes the face
- tears tear - shed a single tear
- tears wipe / tears wipeall - dry this face / every face at once
- tears wipehand - wipe the tears away with a hand gesture
- tears nearby 0.7 30 1500 - every actor within 1500 units of the player cries
- tears status - what Lacrima found on the head and what the tears are doing
- tears ducts 1 - mark where tears start (0 to hide)
- tears streams 3 - this actor's tears start from 3 points per eye (0 = the setting)
- tears mascara 1 - running mascara on this actor (0 none, -1 = the setting)
- tears enable 0 - turn Lacrima off for this session (1 on)


FOR MOD AUTHORS

Lacrima does nothing on its own; it is meant to be driven by your mod. Every call is a global function, so an optional integration needs no master and no property:

    If Lacrima.GetAPIVersion() > 0          ; 0 = Lacrima is not installed
        Lacrima.Cry(akActor, 0.7, 20.0)     ; intensity 0.0 to 1.0, seconds (0 or less = until stopped)
    EndIf

    Lacrima.StopCrying(akActor)             ; the tears on the face dry up
    Lacrima.StopCrying(akActor, true)       ; or vanish at once
    Lacrima.ShedTear(akActor)               ; one tear, also when not crying
    Lacrima.WipeTears(akActor)
    Lacrima.WipeTearsWithHand(akActor)      ; optional second argument: your own Idle
    Bool crying = Lacrima.IsCrying(akActor)

    If Lacrima.GetAPIVersion() >= 2         ; per-actor overrides, for the session
        Lacrima.SetStreams(akActor, 3)      ; tears from 3 points per eye (0 = the setting)
        Lacrima.SetMascara(akActor, 1.0)    ; running mascara (0 = none, negative = the setting)
    EndIf

Mod events, with the crying actor as the sender, so an expression mod can react to crying it did not start:
- Lacrima_CryStart (numArg = intensity)
- Lacrima_CryStop

SKSE plugins get the same calls as C exports of Lacrima.dll (include/API/LacrimaAPI.h in the source); there is nothing to link, and every call is safe from any thread.

Crying does not survive loading a save, and no Lacrima_CryStop is sent then.
