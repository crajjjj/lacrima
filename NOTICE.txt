Lacrima contains code adapted from OStrands by LeonVanWinkle (Saint), also
GPL-3.0, taken from OStrands 0.8.0 (https://gitlab.com/LilRicefield/ostrands):

- src/fluid/*: the position-based fluid solver, skin lookup grid, clusters and
  density-field surface. The solver is adapted to a frame that moves with the head:
  gravity is a vector, the ground heightfield is gone, a sorted cell grid replaces
  the hash maps, one neighbour search serves a whole solve, and fluid at rest on a
  surface sleeps. Skin contact takes front faces only, keeps reaching a particle
  briefly after it loses the skin, and holds a drop under a surface that faces down
  for a while; adhesion falls with the fluid around a particle. Clusters and surface
  are unchanged apart from the namespace and caller-owned output buffers.
- src/GeometryMath.h: the rope frame transport, unchanged apart from the namespace.
- src/Face.cpp: the skin wrap (vertex decoding, facegen dynamic data, skinning),
  reduced from OStrands' whole-body wrap to the face, posed only where the fluid is,
  with tear duct placement added.
- src/Tears.cpp: the fluid rendering (blobs, ropes, drops), with emitters,
  reservoirs and ground sampling replaced by tear ducts, in the head bone's frame.
- src/Hooks.cpp: the frame driver.
- tools/nifkit.py and tools/make_tears.py: the NIF and DDS writers.

vendor/SKSEMenuFramework/SKSEMenuFramework.h is the client header of SKSE Menu
Framework, GPL-3.0; see vendor/SKSEMenuFramework/README.txt.
