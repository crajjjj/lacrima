Lacrima contains code adapted from OStrands by LeonVanWinkle (Saint), also
GPL-3.0, taken from OStrands 0.8.0 (https://gitlab.com/LilRicefield/ostrands):

- src/fluid/*: the position-based fluid solver, skin lookup grid, clusters and
  density-field surface, unchanged apart from the namespace.
- src/GeometryMath.h: the rope frame transport, unchanged apart from the namespace.
- src/Face.cpp: the skin wrap (vertex decoding, facegen dynamic data, skinning),
  reduced from OStrands' whole-body wrap to the face and eyes, with tear duct
  placement added.
- src/Tears.cpp: the fluid rendering (blobs, ropes, drops), with emitters,
  reservoirs and ground sampling replaced by tear ducts.
- src/Hooks.cpp: the frame driver.
- tools/nifkit.py and tools/make_tears.py: the NIF and DDS writers.

vendor/SKSEMenuFramework/SKSEMenuFramework.h is the client header of SKSE Menu
Framework, GPL-3.0; see vendor/SKSEMenuFramework/README.txt.
